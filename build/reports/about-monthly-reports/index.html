About Monthly Reports

January 1, 2026

Welcome to Monthly Reports! This section tracks Project Bluefin's development activity, completed work, and contributor highlights from todo.projectbluefin.io.

What's in a report?​

Each monthly report includes:

  • Completed work from the project board (issues and pull requests moved to "Done")
  • Contributor highlights recognizing everyone who contributed during the month
  • Focus areas showing activity organized by project area (Desktop, Development, Ecosystem, Hardware, Infrastructure)

Report Schedule​

Reports are published automatically on the first Monday of each month

Looking for OS releases?​

These reports track project activity and development progress. For OS version updates, check out the Changelogs page. For announcements and deep dives, visit the Blog.

Reports are generated automatically from todo.projectbluefin.io. View the board or report an issue.
